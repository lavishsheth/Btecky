Quick setup

How to find your public profile URL

Profile fetching works only when Google Skills can show your achievements publicly.

1

Open Google Skills

Visit Google Skills and sign in to the account you use for the Arcade program.

Open Google Skills ↗
2

Enable public visibility

Open your profile or account settings and enable the option to share your profile publicly.

3

Copy your profile link

Copy the public profile URL and paste it into the calculator above. Do not share your password or private account link.

Profile activation

Private profiles cannot be read by this calculator. Change visibility to Public before fetching.

Badge sync latency

New completions may take time to appear on your public profile. If a badge is missing, refresh later and calculate again.

Duplicate filtering

Repeated badge titles are deduplicated before scoring. Repeating the same badge does not create extra points under the calculator's unique-title rule.

Privacy: B Tecky requests only the public profile page. Never enter a Google password, session cookie, access token or other credential. The profile URL is saved only in your browser if you choose to use the browser's autofill.

Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Why are my new badges not showing?

Google Skills may take time to synchronize recently completed activities. Refresh your public profile later and run the calculation again.

Why is my points total lower than expected?

Check that your profile is public, that the correct account is being used, and that the badge appears on the public page. Duplicate titles are filtered and only activities that can be identified as eligible are counted.

Are lab-free courses counted?

This calculator counts only activities that can be matched to the configured eligible Arcade rules. It does not assume that every course completion earns Arcade points.

What is the Facilitator bonus?

The 2026 Facilitator Program ran from July 13 to September 14, 2026. Eligible participants could earn milestone bonuses by meeting the published game and skill-badge requirements, plus the separate AI agent bonus milestone.

Is this an official Google calculator?

No. B Tecky is an independent community resource and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC or Google Cloud. Official Google records and announcements take precedence.

Does B Tecky store my Google password or profile data?

No Google password, authentication token or private account access is requested. The calculator fetches the public profile URL submitted by you. The profile URL is saved only in this browser's local storage for convenience; it is sent to the read-only fetch endpoint only when you click Calculate.
