Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator automatically read my Google Skills profile?

No. This clean rebuild uses manual counts so it does not depend on fragile page scraping, browser sessions, Google login access or a third-party profile parser. You can check your public profile separately and enter your eligible totals.

Are all badges on my profile eligible?

No. Only badges and activities recognized by the current Arcade season rules count. Old, deprecated, duplicate or unrelated credentials may not qualify.

Can I include a Facilitator bonus?

Yes, if you are an eligible Facilitator and have official confirmation of the milestone. Select the highest verified milestone only. The calculator does not decide eligibility.

Are prize tiers guaranteed?

No. Thresholds indicate score bands. Prize inventory, timing, eligibility, regional rules and the official Prize Counter process may affect rewards.

Does the calculator save my progress?

Your entered counts are saved locally in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded to B Tecky. Use Reset calculator to clear them.

Is B Tecky affiliated with Google?

No. B Tecky is an independent community resource and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC or Google Cloud.
