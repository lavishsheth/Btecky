      Questions people ask

      Is this calculator official?

      No. It is an independent tool made by B Tecky and is not connected to Google. It works from the published rules, so your official total on Google Skills and in your Arcade Insider email is the one that counts.

      Why do I type my numbers instead of pasting a profile link?

      Some calculators read your public Google Skills profile from a server. This one is a plain web page with no server, so nothing about you leaves your device. You either type your numbers or tick your badges in the checklist, and it remembers them only in your own browser.

      Which tier am I in if I have exactly 95 points?

      Champion. Each tier starts at its listed number: Trooper 50, Ranger 75, Champion 95 and Legend 120. Points such as 94.5 are still in the tier below.

      What is the waterfall system?

      Each tier has a limited number of prizes: 2,500 for Legend, 3,000 for Champion, 4,000 for Ranger and 6,000 for Trooper. If your tier is full, your points move you down to the next tier instead of losing them. The earlier you reach your points, the better your place in the line.

      Do skill badges still count after the Facilitator program ended?

      Milestones and Facilitator bonus points were locked when the program ended on 14 September 2026. Community trackers report that badges finished later still count as normal Arcade points, which is how this calculator treats them.

      What if my number is different from Google Skills?

      Compare your counts first, then use the "Other Arcade points" box for anything this calculator does not cover, such as points from earlier in the year. If it is still different, trust Google Skills and check the official Arcade page.
