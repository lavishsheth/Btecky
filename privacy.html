Who runs this site

This site is run by Lavish, the creator of the B Tecky YouTube channel. Contact: lavishsheth122@gmail.com.

The Arcade Score Checker

When you type your email into the score checker, it is processed only inside your browser. It is turned into a one-way fingerprint and compared with a list of fingerprints that ships with the page. Your email is not sent to any server, not stored, and not logged by this site. The list of fingerprints does not contain readable email addresses.

Results show a partly hidden name, points, milestone status, rank, and the names of completed Arcade games and skill badges, taken from a Facilitator program report. If you would like your entry removed, email the address above and it will be taken out of the next update.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage or opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

YouTube videos

The site shows videos and playlists from the B Tecky YouTube channel. Video players use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube may set cookies when you play a video. Channel information is loaded from the YouTube Data API. See Google's privacy policy.

Blog

The blog is made of static pages. There are no comment forms or accounts, and this site does not collect personal information from blog readers.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads.

Not an official Google site

B Tecky is an independent creator site. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Points shown are estimates; your official balance is on Google Skills.

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
