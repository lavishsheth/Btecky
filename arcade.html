      Questions people ask

      Which email should I enter?

      Use the email address linked to your Google Skills account when you joined the Facilitator program. Capital letters do not matter, and for Gmail addresses dots and +tags are ignored.

      My email says "not found". What now?

      Check for typos first. This page only lists people from the two Facilitator batches in the report dated 18 September 2026. If you joined through another batch or after that date, you will not appear yet.

      Why is my score different from Google Skills?

      This page estimates points from the published rules and the report snapshot. Google Skills shows the official balance, including anything earned after the report date and any points from earlier in the year.

      Is my email stored or shown to others?

      No. The list behind this page contains only one-way fingerprints of emails, not the emails themselves. Your entry is turned into a fingerprint in your own browser and compared locally, so nothing you type leaves your device. Names are shown partly hidden.
