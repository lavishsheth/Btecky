What Is Build with Google Gemini 2026?

Build with Google Gemini is a fully virtual hackathon designed to encourage hands-on adoption of Gemini Enterprise and the Google Cloud AI stack among working professionals in India.

The idea is to bring developers and problem solvers together to address practical challenges faced by Indian enterprises.

Instead of building another basic chatbot or a small demonstration application, participants are expected to choose one of four problem statements and propose a meaningful solution.

The hackathon follows a structured process:

  1. Register individually or form a team.
  2. Choose one of the four problem statements.
  3. Submit your proposed idea.
  4. Wait for the shortlisting process.
  5. If shortlisted, receive $100 in Google Cloud credits.
  6. Build a working prototype using Gemini Enterprise.
  7. Submit a 1–2 minute demonstration video.
  8. Participate in the evaluation process and compete for the prizes.

The program is designed to take participants from an initial idea to a working prototype that can demonstrate how AI can solve real business problems.

Four Problem Statements – What Do You Need to Build?

The hackathon features four problem statements, with Gemini Enterprise at the centre of the solutions.

Participants need to choose one problem statement and submit an idea explaining how they would solve it.

Here is an overview of all four challenges.

Problem Statement 1: Voice-Powered Enterprise Knowledge & Decision Agent

Organizations generate and store large amounts of information across different platforms.

This information may be spread across:

  • Documents.
  • Support tickets.
  • Project wikis.
  • Chat conversations.
  • Enterprise knowledge sources.

Employees often struggle to find the right information quickly, especially when they need to make decisions or respond to business questions.

The challenge is to build a voice-powered enterprise knowledge and decision agent that can help employees access relevant information and make better decisions.

A potential solution could allow employees to ask questions using their voice and receive useful responses based on enterprise knowledge sources.

Think about how Gemini Enterprise can help users retrieve information, understand context, and make knowledge more accessible.

Problem Statement 2: Voice-Driven Agentic Workflow Automation

Many business operations still involve repetitive manual steps.

Examples include:

  • Updating a ledger.
  • Logging a field visit.
  • Booking a service.
  • Filing a claim.
  • Recording a delivery.
  • Capturing a compliance check.

These activities can involve multiple systems and repeated data entry.

The challenge is to develop a voice-driven agentic workflow automation solution that can help simplify these processes.

For example, imagine a field employee describing a completed visit through voice input. An intelligent agent could interpret the request and help initiate the required workflow.

The goal is to explore how voice AI and agentic capabilities can make business operations more efficient.

Problem Statement 3: Customer & Frontline Voice Agents

Customer service representatives, field employees, site operators, and other frontline workers often interact with customers in fast-moving environments.

These conversations may take place in noisy locations or situations where typing is inconvenient.

The challenge focuses on building customer and frontline voice agents that can support these interactions.

A useful solution could help frontline teams understand customer requests, retrieve relevant information, and assist with common service workflows.

Think about how voice-based AI can improve customer experience and support employees who work outside traditional office environments.

Problem Statement 4: Open Innovation – Real-World Voice AI with Google Gemini

The fourth problem statement gives participants an opportunity to explore their own real-world use cases.

Organizations across industries face unique operational challenges and growth opportunities that traditional software and basic chatbots may not solve effectively.

This problem statement encourages participants to identify a meaningful challenge and develop an innovative Voice AI solution using Google Gemini.

Potential areas to explore include:

  • Retail operations.
  • Micro-enterprises.
  • Field operations.
  • Customer engagement.
  • Business process automation.
  • Enterprise productivity.

This is the open-innovation track, so you have more flexibility to identify a problem and propose your own solution.

Tip: Before selecting a problem statement, read its complete description on the official website. The summaries above are introductory explanations, not substitutes for the full technical requirements.

Tips to Build a Strong Hackathon Project

If you want your project to stand out, focus on solving a real problem and demonstrating a working solution.

Here are some practical suggestions.

1. Choose the Right Problem Statement

Do not select a problem statement only because it sounds interesting.

Think about your technical strengths, the problem's complexity, and whether you can build a meaningful prototype within the available timeline.

2. Focus on a Specific Use Case

Avoid trying to build an entire enterprise platform.

Instead, identify one important workflow and solve it well.

For example, under the voice-driven workflow automation problem, you could focus on one specific process such as field visit reporting.

3. Demonstrate Meaningful Gemini Enterprise Usage

The hackathon places 25% weightage on Gemini Enterprise capability.

Make sure your project demonstrates how the technology contributes to the solution.

Explain which capabilities you used and why they are important to your use case.

4. Build a Working Prototype

A clear, functional prototype is more useful than a presentation containing only screenshots and diagrams.

Focus on the main workflow and ensure that your demo can show it working.

5. Prepare a Strong Demo Video

Your prototype submission requires a 1–2 minute demo video.

Use this time carefully:

  • Introduce the problem.
  • Show your solution.
  • Demonstrate the main workflow.
  • Explain where Gemini Enterprise is used.
  • Highlight the practical benefit.

6. Work on Your Presentation

Your idea, prototype, and presentation should tell one consistent story.

Explain the problem first, then demonstrate how your application solves it.
