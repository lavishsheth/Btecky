OPENAI IS LOOKING FOR AMBASSADORS! 🚀 || Codex Ambassador & Student Collective 2026 || Apply Now!

OpenAI is inviting developers and students to explore its Codex Ambassador and Student Collective programs. Discover campus leadership opportunities, community building, project creation, and how to apply!

🚀 OPENAI IS LOOKING FOR AMBASSADORS! 😱 || CODEX AMBASSADOR & STUDENT COLLECTIVE 2026 || APPLY NOW!

Hey developers, students, AI enthusiasts, and tech community leaders! 🎉

OpenAI is bringing exciting opportunities for people who love Artificial Intelligence, building projects, creating content, and helping others explore the latest AI technologies.

If you enjoy experimenting with ChatGPT, building applications using Codex, organizing tech events, and growing developer communities, you should definitely explore these two programs!

OpenAI has two community opportunities worth knowing about:

  1. OpenAI Codex Ambassador Program.
  2. OpenAI Student Collective.

Both programs focus on bringing people together, encouraging AI adoption, and helping developers and students build amazing things using OpenAI tools.

Let's explore the details!

🤖 1. What Is the OpenAI Codex Ambassador Program?

The OpenAI Codex Ambassador Program is a community initiative designed for developers, content creators, event organizers, and experienced Codex users.

The main objective is to bring more developers into the Codex ecosystem and help communities discover what they can build using AI-powered coding tools.

As a Codex Ambassador, you can contribute by:

  • Organizing developer meetups.
  • Hosting Codex workshops.
  • Creating educational content.
  • Publishing tutorials and demonstrations.
  • Sharing your Codex projects.
  • Helping developers explore AI-powered coding.
  • Providing feedback to the OpenAI team.

The program offers two participation paths.

🎤 Path 1: Event Ambassador

If you enjoy organizing events, workshops, and developer meetups, this path may be suitable.

Your responsibilities can include:

  • Organizing local developer meetups.
  • Hosting Codex workshops.
  • Conducting hands-on sessions.
  • Bringing developers together.
  • Helping participants build projects using Codex.

Previous experience organizing technology events is preferred, particularly events with 50 or more attendees.

🎥 Path 2: Content Ambassador

If you love creating YouTube videos, Instagram reels, tutorials, technical blogs, and project demonstrations, this path may be suitable.

You can contribute by:

  • Creating Codex tutorials.
  • Publishing AI development videos.
  • Demonstrating interesting Codex workflows.
  • Sharing projects built using Codex.
  • Creating educational social media content.
  • Explaining new OpenAI features.

You can also apply for both paths if you have experience in event organization and content creation.

🎁 What Do Codex Ambassadors Receive?

OpenAI provides several benefits and resources to selected Codex Ambassadors.

💻 1. ChatGPT Pro Subscription

Selected ambassadors receive a ChatGPT Pro subscription during their program participation.

🚀 2. Codex & API Credits

Ambassadors receive Codex and API credits to explore OpenAI technologies and build projects.

🎁 3. Exclusive OpenAI Merchandise

Selected ambassadors can receive exclusive Codex Ambassador merchandise.

🎤 4. Event Support

For approved and eligible events, OpenAI may provide:

  • Codex and API credits for attendees.
  • Food and refreshments.
  • Hackathon prizes.
  • Custom event merchandise, where possible.

🌍 5. Networking Opportunities

You can connect with the OpenAI team and other ambassadors across different communities.

There may also be opportunities for invitations to official events such as DevDay and Builder Lounges, although these are not guaranteed.

🏅 6. Community Recognition

You get an opportunity to represent the Codex community, grow your professional network, and establish yourself as a community leader.

Important: The Codex Ambassador Program is a voluntary and unpaid six-month initiative. Benefits are program support, not a salary.

👨‍💻 Who Can Apply for Codex Ambassador?

OpenAI looks for people who:

  • Have experience building projects using Codex.
  • Enjoy helping other developers.
  • Have experience creating educational content or organizing events.
  • Can contribute at least once every month.
  • Are interested in AI-powered software development.
  • Can follow OpenAI's community guidelines.

⚠️ Important Eligibility Update for Students

The Codex Ambassador application form specifically states:

OpenAI is not currently accepting Codex Ambassador applications from undergraduate students.

Undergraduate students are encouraged to explore the OpenAI Student Collective instead.

The Codex Ambassador program also requires a six-month commitment and at least one useful contribution every month.

🎓 2. What Is OpenAI Student Collective?

Now comes another exciting opportunity, especially for college students!

OpenAI Student Collective is a campus leadership initiative that encourages undergraduate students to introduce AI technologies to their colleges and help other students learn, experiment, and build projects.

OpenAI is looking for students who are passionate about technology, community building, creativity, and Artificial Intelligence.

As a Campus Lead, you will work in a pair with another selected student to create an AI-focused community at your college.

The objective is to make AI learning accessible to students from different academic backgrounds.

You don't need to be an expert in Artificial Intelligence to apply!

🚀 What Will You Do as an OpenAI Campus Lead?

The Student Collective focuses on four major activities.

1. Workshops

Organize welcoming workshops where students learn about ChatGPT, Codex, and other OpenAI tools.

The program plans four workshops per semester.

2. Studio Hours

Conduct weekly collaborative sessions where students can work on their AI projects, ask questions, and learn from each other.

3. Showcases

Organize campus-wide showcases where students demonstrate the applications and projects they have built using AI.

These showcases take place once per semester.

4. Community Building

Create a welcoming environment where students from different departments can learn, collaborate, and explore AI together.

The community remains active throughout the program.

🎁 What Will OpenAI Student Collective Members Receive?

OpenAI provides several benefits to selected Campus Leads.

💻 1. ChatGPT Subscription

Receive a ChatGPT subscription during the program.

🚀 2. Codex Credits

Get access to Codex credits to explore AI-powered coding and build projects.

💰 3. Campus Event Funding

OpenAI provides funding and resources to help Campus Leads organize workshops and student activities.

🎁 4. Exclusive Merchandise

Receive merchandise to support campus events and community activities.

🎓 5. Training & Guidance

Get access to training materials, workshop guides, and resources from OpenAI.

🌍 6. Global Networking

Connect with other Campus Leads and get opportunities to interact with the OpenAI team.

💵 7. Cash Stipend Every Semester

One of the most interesting benefits!

Campus Leads receive a cash stipend each semester in recognition of their time and leadership.

The exact stipend details are shared during onboarding.

✈️ 8. Potential OpenAI Headquarters Visit

Selected Campus Leads may also have an opportunity for an OpenAI HQ visit in June 2027.

This opportunity is subject to the program's arrangements and conditions.

📋 How to Apply for OpenAI Codex Ambassador?

If you are eligible, follow these steps:

Step 1: Visit the official Codex Ambassador application page.

Step 2: Enter your personal details.

Step 3: Describe your previous ambassador experience, if any.

Step 4: Explain why you want to become a Codex Ambassador.

Step 5: Share your favourite project built using Codex.

Step 6: Select your preferred path:

  • Event Ambassador.
  • Content Ambassador.
  • Both.

Step 7: Share links to your previous events, content, GitHub, LinkedIn, or personal website.

Step 8: Prepare an approximately 30-second introduction video.

Your video can be in any language and does not need to be public, but anyone with the link must be able to view it.

Step 9: Review the six-month commitment and program guidelines before submitting.

What Should Your Introduction Video Include?

Your 30-second video should briefly explain:

  • Who you are.
  • Your technical interests.
  • What you have built using Codex.
  • Your experience creating content or organizing events.
  • How you would contribute to the OpenAI community.

Keep it natural, clear, and engaging.

Application status: The OpenAI developer program page currently indicates that Codex Ambassador applications are closed. Check the application page for any reopening.

📝 How to Apply for OpenAI Student Collective?

Undergraduate students can explore the Student Collective through the following process.

Step 1: Visit the Official Student Collective Website

Open the OpenAI Student Collective page.

Step 2: Click Apply or Register Your Interest

Check whether applications are currently available for your country.

Step 3: Complete Your Application

Provide your academic information, contact details, and relevant community experience.

Step 4: Explain Your Campus Ideas

Describe how you would bring students together and introduce them to ChatGPT and Codex.

Step 5: Share Your Interest in Community Building

Explain how you would organize workshops, weekly Studio Hours, and student project showcases.

Step 6: Submit Your Application

Complete the application and wait for further communication from OpenAI.

India update: Applications for the Fall 2026 cohort in India have closed. However, OpenAI allows interested students to register their interest for consideration in future cohorts.
