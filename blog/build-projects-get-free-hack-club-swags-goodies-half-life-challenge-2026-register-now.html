🔧 What Is Hack Club Half Life 2026?

Hack Club Half Life is a hands-on hardware learning program organized by Hack Club, a nonprofit community that encourages teenagers to learn programming, electronics, and engineering by building real projects.

Unlike traditional online courses, this program focuses on practical learning.

Participants spend 10 weeks designing and building hardware projects with guidance from the Hack Club community.

The program is designed for beginners as well as students who already have experience with electronics and hardware development.

You don't need previous hardware experience to participate!

🔥 What Will You Build During the 10-Week Challenge?

The Half Life program is divided into two major phases.

Phase 1: Design Your Projects – First 5 Weeks

During the first five weeks, participants explore different hardware concepts and design five projects.

Here are the main weekly themes:

Week 1: PCBs

Learn about Printed Circuit Boards and design your own PCB-based projects.

Week 2: CAD

Explore Computer-Aided Design and create designs for physical hardware components.

Week 3: Music

Experiment with electronics and create projects involving sound and music.

Week 4: Displays

Learn about displays and build projects involving visual output and electronic interfaces.

Week 5: Breadboard Logic

Understand digital electronics, logic circuits, and how different components work together.

Phase 2: Build Your Projects – Next 5 Weeks

Once you complete the design phase, it's time to turn your ideas into real hardware!

During the next five weeks, you will work on building your projects using the components and materials required.

You can experiment, test your designs, improve your circuits, and create functional hardware prototypes.

The goal is to learn by building!
