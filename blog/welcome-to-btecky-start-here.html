Welcome to B Tecky: what the channel covers and where to start

Meet the B Tecky YouTube channel and its official website: what I make, who it is for, and the best places to start.

Hi, I'm Lavish, a computer science student pursuing a B-Tech degree, and I'm passionate about technology. B Tecky is my YouTube channel, and this website is its official home.

What the channel covers

  • Google Cloud and the Qwiklabs program
  • Coverage of events and programs, including the Google Cloud Arcade
  • Programming videos, tips and tricks
  • Interviews and internship advice, and more

The goal is simple: useful content you can apply to your own learning, whether you are collecting skill badges or preparing for your first tech job.

Where to start

  1. Watch the videos. The latest uploads and playlists appear in the videos section of the home page, and you can subscribe on YouTube.
  2. Read the blog. Guides and news, like how Arcade points work and a free Google Cloud roadmap.
  3. Try the tools. The Arcade Score Checker shows your Facilitator report score, and the Arcade Points Calculator works out your points and prize tier.
