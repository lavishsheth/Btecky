🤖 What Is Naukri Campus AI Quiz Challenge 2026?

Naukri Campus, in collaboration with IES University, Bhopal, is hosting an exciting online competition called:

What's Your AI-Q? – Daily AI Quiz Challenge

The objective is simple: test your Artificial Intelligence knowledge by answering five short questions every day.

Participants earn points for correct answers and compete with students across India through a leaderboard.

The challenge focuses on different areas of Artificial Intelligence, including:

  • Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  • Machine Learning (ML)
  • Deep Learning
  • Generative AI
  • Computer Vision
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP)
  • AI Ethics
  • Prompt Engineering

You don't need to be an AI expert to participate. Students interested in learning about AI can also take part!

📋 How to Participate in the AI Quiz Challenge 2026?

The participation process is simple.

Follow these steps:

Step 1: Visit the Official Naukri Campus AI Quiz Page

Open the official AI Quiz Challenge page using the registration link provided below.

Step 2: Register for the Challenge

Sign in or create your Naukri Campus account.

Complete the registration process with the required details.

Step 3: Start Playing the Daily AI Quiz

Once registered, start attempting the daily quiz.

You need to answer 5 short questions every day.

Step 4: Earn Points

You will earn:

  • 1 point for every correct answer.
  • Additional quiz opportunities through the referral and extra-life mechanism, as described in the contest instructions.

Step 5: Climb the National Leaderboard

Keep participating and try to maintain a high score.

Your performance will determine your leaderboard position.

Step 6: Win Exciting Gadgets!

Participants securing the eligible top positions can win the announced rewards, including a MacBook Neo, Meta AI Glasses, robot toys, and goodies.
