🤖 What Is Agentforce Service Agents?

Agentforce is Salesforce's platform for building AI agents that can assist customers and employees.

Agentforce Service Agents are designed to help organizations improve customer service by combining human expertise with AI-powered assistance.

These agents can help with activities such as:

  • Answering customer questions.
  • Handling common service requests.
  • Supporting customer service representatives.
  • Providing intelligent recommendations.
  • Improving service workflows.
  • Delivering more personalized customer experiences.

Through this Quest, you'll learn the fundamentals of Agentforce and understand how AI agents can work alongside human service teams.
