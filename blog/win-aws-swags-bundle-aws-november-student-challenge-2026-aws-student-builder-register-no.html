☁️ What Is the AWS November Student Challenge 2026?

AWS Builder Center has introduced an exciting hackathon called Strands SDK: Workshop to Ship.

The challenge encourages developers to learn about AI agents through a dedicated hands-on workshop and use their knowledge to create original AI-powered solutions that deliver meaningful social impact.

Participants will work with the Strands Agents SDK, an open-source framework for building AI agents.

The goal is simple:

  • Learn how to build AI agents.
  • Complete the official Strands SDK workshop.
  • Develop an original AI-powered project.
  • Solve a real-world problem.
  • Submit your project on AWS Builder Center.
  • Compete for exciting AWS rewards!

Whether you're interested in AI development, cloud computing, or building socially impactful applications, this is a great opportunity to showcase your skills.

🤖 What Do You Need to Build?

This challenge is not just about completing labs or collecting badges.

You need to build and ship an original AI agent using the Strands Agents SDK that addresses a real-world social good problem.

AWS has highlighted three major focus areas:

1. 🎓 Education

Build AI-powered solutions that improve learning opportunities.

Some project ideas include:

  • AI-powered personal tutor.
  • Mock interview preparation assistant.
  • AI reading assistant for students.
  • Personalized practice question generator.

2. ❤️ Health

Develop AI solutions that improve healthcare accessibility and support.

Some ideas include:

  • AI-powered mental health check-in assistant.
  • Accessibility assistant for visually impaired users.
  • Medication information assistant.
  • Healthcare guidance chatbot.

3. 🌍 Climate Resilience

Use AI to address environmental challenges and help communities prepare for climate-related risks.

Some ideas include:

  • Heatwave and drought early-warning assistant.
  • Flood preparedness assistant.
  • Nearby cooling center locator.
  • AI-powered climate information assistant for farmers.

Choose a meaningful problem and build something that can create measurable positive impact for people or the planet.

📋 How to Participate in the AWS Challenge 2026?

Follow these steps to get started.

Step 1: Visit AWS Builder Center

Open the official AWS Builder Center challenge page and sign in using your account.

Step 2: Join the Hackathon

Find the Strands SDK: Workshop to Ship challenge and click the Join option.

You must join the hackathon before creating your project post.

Step 3: Complete the Free Strands SDK Workshop

This is an important requirement!

Complete the official workshop:

Building AI Agents with Strands SDK: From Simple Loops to Multi-Agent Swarms

To access it:

  1. Visit the free sandbox workshops listing on AWS Builder Center.
  2. Find the Strands SDK workshop.
  3. Click Request Free Sandbox Environment.
  4. Complete the workshop during the hackathon period.

Workshop completion is mandatory to qualify.

Step 4: Build Your AI Agent

Use the Strands Agents SDK to develop an original AI agent.

Your project must address a real-world social good problem in one of the three focus areas:

  • Education
  • Health
  • Climate Resilience

Focus on originality, functionality, and measurable impact.

Step 5: Prepare Your Project Submission

Your submission must include:

  1. An original AI agent built using the Strands Agents SDK.
  2. A project article on AWS Builder Center explaining your solution and the social problem it addresses.
  3. A link to your publicly accessible source-code repository.
  4. A public demo video of less than 3 minutes showing your AI agent in action.

Important: Your agent must be original and must not have been published previously.

Step 6: Submit Your Project

Complete your project submission on AWS Builder Center before the deadline.

Make sure all the required links and project details are included.

AWS will evaluate submissions in two rounds.

Round 1: AI and Human Scoring

All qualifying submissions will be evaluated, and the top 50 projects will advance to the next round.

Round 2: Human Judging

A panel of AWS experts will review the shortlisted projects and select the final three winners.
