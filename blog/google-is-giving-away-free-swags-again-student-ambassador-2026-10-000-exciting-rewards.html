🚀 What Is Google Gemini Student Ambassador Program 2026?

The Google Gemini Student Ambassador Program is a campus leadership initiative designed to help students explore Artificial Intelligence and introduce Google Gemini to their college communities.

As a Student Ambassador, you will represent Gemini on your campus and encourage students to discover how AI can improve learning, productivity, creativity, and everyday tasks.

The program offers opportunities to:

  • Represent Google Gemini at your college.
  • Organize AI workshops and campus activities.
  • Introduce students to Google's AI tools.
  • Build leadership and communication skills.
  • Connect with student ambassadors across India.
  • Earn exciting rewards through program activities.

Semester 2 registrations are open on the official program website.

📝 How to Apply for Google Student Ambassador 2026?

Follow these steps to complete your registration.

Step 1: Visit the Official Website

Open the Google Gemini Student Ambassador Program website.

You will find information about the program, rewards, eligibility, and the application process.

Step 2: Click on Apply Now

Find the Apply Now option on the official website.

This will take you to the student ambassador recruitment form.

Step 3: Enter Your Personal Details

Complete the application form with accurate information.

The form requests details such as:

  • Full name
  • Gmail email address
  • Mobile number
  • College or university
  • State
  • Course or branch
  • Current year of study
  • Ambassador GID, where applicable
  • Social media profiles

Step 4: Complete the Application Questions

The recruitment process includes questions designed to understand your interests, campus knowledge, communication skills, and ideas.

Take your time and provide thoughtful, genuine responses.

Step 5: Submit Your Application

Review your details carefully and submit the form.

The application process is estimated to take approximately 10–12 minutes.

Step 6: Attend the Video Interview If Shortlisted

Students who clear the initial application round may be invited to record a short video interview.

Use this opportunity to explain your interest in AI, your campus community, and how you would promote Google Gemini.

Step 7: Complete Onboarding If Selected

Selected students will receive further instructions to complete onboarding and begin their ambassador journey.

🤖 What Will You Do as a Google Student Ambassador?

As an ambassador, your role is to help more students discover and use Google Gemini.

Some potential activities include:

1. Organize Gemini Workshops

Help students understand how Gemini can assist with learning, research, coding, and productivity.

3. Build Your Campus Community

4. Participate in Product Trials

Explore new AI experiences and provide useful feedback through official activities.

5. Complete Ambassador Challenges

Participate in assigned activities to accumulate points and progress through the reward system.

🏅 How Does the Google Ambassador Reward System Work?

The program follows a performance-based reward structure.

Here is the general process:

  1. Get selected for the ambassador program.
  2. Complete the official onboarding.
  3. Participate in assigned activities.
  4. Earn points through qualifying contributions.
  5. Progress through the reward tiers.
  6. Become eligible for rewards based on your achievements.

Remember: Not every participant will receive every reward. Higher-value rewards may require significant activity, leaderboard performance, or selection through a competition.
