🎁 EARN GOOGLE SWAGS! 🔥 GFG × Google Cloud 2026 — Complete A to Z Guide

Want to learn Google Cloud for free, earn Google Skill Badges, and get a chance to win exclusive goodies? 👀 The GeeksforGeeks × Google Cloud Learn with Communities 2026 campaign gives students and developers access to hands-on cloud labs, learning credits, curated learning tracks, and rewards for completing the requir

☁️ What Is GFG × Google Cloud Learn with Communities?

This campaign is a collaboration between GeeksforGeeks and Google Cloud designed to help students and developers build practical cloud computing skills.

Instead of only watching tutorials, you'll use Google Skills to complete hands-on labs and earn skill badges.

🎯 What Can You Get?

☁️ 9 Google Skills credits after redeeming your access code

🎁 200 additional free credits after completing your first hands-on lab

🏅 Google Cloud Skill Badges

📜 An exclusive Google-branded certificate for completing at least five skill badges

🎁 A chance to win GeeksforGeeks-exclusive swag

💻 Practical experience with cloud technologies

📈 A stronger public Google Skills profile

Remember: The certificate and swag are different rewards. Swag is advertised as a chance to win, not a guaranteed gift for everyone who registers.

🎁 Does Everyone Get Guaranteed Google Swags?

Let's clear up the biggest doubt!

So, don't register expecting an automatic swag delivery.

The main goal is to complete the learning activities and follow the reward-claim process correctly.

🚀 How to Register — Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Create a New Google Skills Account

Visit Google Skills and create a new account using a fresh email address.

The campaign requires an eligible new Google Skills account created after September 14, 2026. The GfG campaign page advises using an account created after September 15, so follow that stricter requirement.

Make sure your Google Skills profile is set to Public, so your badges can be verified.

Step 2: Complete the GeeksforGeeks Enrolment Form

Visit the official GfG × Google Cloud enrolment form.

While filling it out:

Select your correct participant category.

Choose GeeksforGeeks as your partner.

Enter the referral code: NRVC72

Provide the correct email associated with your new Google Skills account.

Developers must also provide the required developer profile information.

⚠️ Important: You can enrol through only one partner for this campaign. Multiple partner submissions can cause the latest submission to be treated as the valid one.

Step 3: Wait for Your Access Code

After your enrolment is processed, you'll receive a personal access code.

The GfG page advises checking approximately two days after registering because codes are generated in batches.

You can also use the official GfG access-code page.

Step 4: Redeem Your Credits

Follow the redemption instructions and use your personal access code.

Once redeemed, you should receive the campaign's starting Google Skills credits.

Never share your personal access code with anyone else.

Step 5: Start Completing Your Labs

Open the recommended learning track and start completing the Google Cloud labs.

Each completed course or activity can help you progress toward earning the required skill badges.

🏅 The Badge Rush: Don't Miss the Dates!

The official campaign page advertises a special Badge Rush activity.

The listed reward requirements are:

Complete five Google Cloud Skill Badges between October 16 and October 21, 2026.

Publish a public LinkedIn post about your five badges.

Copy the URL of your published post.

Submit the official reward-claim form.

Wait for the campaign team to verify your submission.

The campaign page says verified rewards should arrive within 15–20 business days after verification.

Since the Badge Rush window is short, check the official campaign page for the latest requirements and availability.

👥 Which Track Should You Choose?

The campaign provides two learning tracks.

🎓 Student Track

Suitable for students who want to build a foundation in cloud computing.

You'll work through beginner-friendly concepts and a curated set of Google Cloud Skill Badges.

💻 Developer Track

Designed for aspiring and working developers who want to explore a more advanced learning path.

Developers must also complete the additional GEAR program step and provide their Google Developer profile URL as instructed by the campaign.

Choose the track that accurately describes you. Don't select a category just because you think it offers better rewards.
