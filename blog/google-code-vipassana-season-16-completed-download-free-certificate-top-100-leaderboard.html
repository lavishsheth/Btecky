🔥 What Is Google Code Vipassana?

Code Vipassana is a Google developer learning initiative that brings developers together through technical learning sessions, hands-on activities, and community participation.

The initiative provides opportunities to learn about different technologies, understand practical use cases, and build projects using Google technologies.

Participants can explore technical concepts through sessions and hands-on activities while interacting with other developers and community members.

Each season focuses on a particular theme, allowing participants to explore a specific area of technology.

🚀 Code Vipassana Season 16 – Data & AI for the Air-gapped Systems

Season 16 focused on the theme:

"Data & AI for the Air-gapped Systems"

🎓 How to Download Your Code Vipassana Season 16 Certificate?

This is the most important part for participants!

Google has provided a dedicated certificate portal where eligible participants can access their season certificates.

Follow these steps:

Step 1: Visit the Official Certificate Portal

Open the certificate download website:

https://certificate.codevipassana.dev/

Step 2: Check Your Certificate

Follow the instructions on the portal to locate your Season 16 certificate.

Step 3: Download Your Certificate

If you are eligible, download the available certificate and save it to your device.

Step 4: Add It to Your LinkedIn Profile

Once you have downloaded your certificate, you can share your achievement with your professional network.

You can also add it to the certifications or accomplishments section of your LinkedIn profile.

Important: According to Google's email, the certificate is available for participants who submitted their results before 5 November 2026. If you did not submit your results before the stated cutoff, the email does not confirm certificate eligibility.
