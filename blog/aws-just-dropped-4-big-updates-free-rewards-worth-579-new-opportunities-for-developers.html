☁️ What Is AWS Builder Center?

AWS Builder Center is the official community platform for developers and cloud enthusiasts.

It brings together everything you need to grow as a developer in one place.

You can:

  • Discover AWS hackathons and build challenges.
  • Explore technical articles and tutorials.
  • Follow experienced developers and AWS experts.
  • Join developer communities.
  • Access hands-on workshops.
  • Learn about new AWS technologies.
  • Earn badges and unlock student rewards.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced developer, AWS Builder Center offers resources to help you improve your skills.

🔥 UPDATE 1: Explore AWS Hackathons & Build Challenges

The first major update is for developers who love building projects and participating in hackathons!

AWS Builder Center has a dedicated Hackathons section where you can discover different building challenges.

These challenges allow developers to work on real-world problems, experiment with AWS services, and showcase their creativity.

What Can You Do?

  • Discover ongoing AWS hackathons.
  • Participate in individual or team challenges.
  • Build AI-powered applications.

  • Submit your projects for evaluation.
  • Compete for exciting prizes and AWS rewards.

Why Should You Participate?

Hackathons are an excellent way to gain practical development experience.

You can build portfolio projects, improve your technical skills, collaborate with other developers, and get recognition from the AWS community.

Pro Tip: Regularly check the Hackathons section because new challenges and competitions are added throughout the year.

What Can You Do With the Mobile App?

👨‍🎓 Who Is Eligible for AWS Student Rewards?

To participate, you generally need to:

  • Be at least 18 years old.
  • Be currently enrolled at an eligible university or higher education institution.
  • Have an AWS Builder Center account.
  • Complete student verification through SheerID.
  • Complete your Builder Center profile.

The program is available in eligible countries and regions. Check the official eligibility requirements before applying.

📋 How to Claim AWS Student Rewards?

Follow these steps:

Step 1: Visit AWS Builder Center.

Step 2: Sign in using your AWS Builder ID.

Step 3: Open the Student Rewards section.

Step 4: Complete your student verification through SheerID.

Step 5: Complete your Builder Center profile by adding your profile picture and About section.

Step 6: Unlock your 12-month Skill Builder Premium subscription.

Step 7: Start earning badges by publishing articles, commenting, and participating in community activities.

Step 8: Reach 7, 14, and 21 badges to unlock the additional rewards.

Remember, you need to complete the required milestones to claim the corresponding rewards.

🧪 UPDATE 4: Free AWS Workshops Sandbox – Learn Without an AWS Account!

This is one of the most useful updates for beginners!

AWS Builder Center now offers free sandbox environments for eligible hands-on workshops.

Previously, many developers used their personal AWS accounts to follow practical tutorials. This could create concerns about unexpected AWS charges.

With the new sandbox experience, you can complete selected workshops using a pre-provisioned AWS environment.

What Is AWS Workshop Sandbox?

An AWS sandbox is a temporary, pre-configured AWS environment where you can experiment with AWS services and complete practical exercises.

Benefits of Free AWS Sandbox

  • No personal AWS account required for the sandbox.
  • No credit card required.
  • No concerns about unexpected charges from your own AWS account during sandbox use.
  • Pre-configured learning environment.
  • Guided hands-on tutorials.
  • Automatic resource cleanup.

⏰ Important Sandbox Limits

How to Access Free AWS Sandbox?

  1. Open AWS Builder Center.
  2. Visit the Workshops section.
  3. Find a workshop that supports free sandbox access.
  4. Click Request Free Sandbox Environment.
  5. Wait for the environment to be provisioned.
  6. Start completing your hands-on AWS exercises!

Note: Not every workshop necessarily offers a sandbox. Look for the free sandbox option on eligible workshops.
