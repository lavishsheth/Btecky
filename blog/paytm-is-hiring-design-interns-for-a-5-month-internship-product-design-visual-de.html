Your Portfolio Matters!

If you're applying for a design internship, don't just submit a portfolio containing screenshots of your final designs.

Try to show: Problem → Research → Ideas → Exploration → Iterations → Final Design

For example: Problem: Users struggle to understand a particular product flow.

Research: Identify the user pain points. Exploration: Create multiple possible solutions. Iteration: Take feedback and improve the design. Final: Present the finished experience and explain why you made those decisions.

This helps demonstrate how you think, not just how you make things look.

Paytm describes its work as building technology-led products for consumers and small businesses, and its careers page emphasizes learning, collaboration and working on products used at scale.

Real product requirements Design critiques Cross-functional collaboration User-focused problem solving Design systems Product development Professional feedback Working with experienced designers

Don't Wait Until the Last Moment If you're eligible and interested, prepare these things before applying:

✅ Resume - Keep it concise and design-focused. ✅ Portfolio - Show 2–4 strong projects instead of dozens of unfinished projects.

✅ Figma Skills - Make sure you're comfortable with components, auto layout, prototyping and basic design systems.

✅ Case Studies - Explain your design decisions instead of only showing final screens.

✅ LinkedIn - Keep your LinkedIn profile updated with your projects and portfolio.
