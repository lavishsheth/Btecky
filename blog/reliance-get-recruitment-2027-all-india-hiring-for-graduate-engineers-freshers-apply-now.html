🎓 Eligibility Criteria

Before applying, carefully check the eligibility requirements.

1. Educational Qualification

Candidates should be pursuing a full-time B.E./B.Tech degree from an AICTE-approved institution in India.

2. Eligible Engineering Branches

The recruitment listing includes the following disciplines:

Chemical Engineering

Mechanical Engineering

Electrical Engineering

Fire Engineering

Civil Engineering

Instrumentation Engineering

Computer Science and IT students are not listed among the eligible branches for this recruitment.

3. Graduation Year

Applicants must belong to the 2027 graduating batch.

4. Academic Requirements

The listing specifies:

Minimum 60% or 6 CGPA in Class 10.

Minimum 60% or 6 CGPA in Class 12 and Diploma, where applicable.

Minimum 60% or 6 CGPA aggregate in engineering up to the latest semester.

5. Backlogs and Education Gap

Candidates with active backlogs or a history of academic detention are not eligible under the published criteria.

The listing also specifies restrictions on education gaps. Review the complete recruitment details before submitting your application.

📝 Reliance GET 2027 Selection Process

The recruitment listing describes a multi-stage selection process.

Step 1: Online Registration

Complete the application through the recruitment portal.

Step 2: Screening

The Reliance team reviews applications to check eligibility and shortlist candidates.

Step 3: Online Assessment

Shortlisted candidates proceed to an online assessment comprising:

Cognitive Test

Subject Matter assessment

The assessment instructions specify that candidates must take the test alone in a quiet, private environment.

Step 4: Additional Details

Shortlisted candidates may need to submit additional information to confirm their candidature.

Step 5: Interview

Candidates who clear the earlier stages may be invited to interviews at designated regional offices.

Step 6: Offer Acceptance

Selected candidates can review and accept their offer through the recruitment portal.

The exact dates and progression depend on the official recruitment schedule and the candidate's selection status.
