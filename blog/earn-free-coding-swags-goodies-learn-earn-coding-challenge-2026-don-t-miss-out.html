What if your daily DSA practice could help you earn exclusive coding merchandise and goodies too? Sounds interesting, right?

TakeUforward has a rewards system where you can earn coins by completing eligible activities and redeem those coins for exciting goodies from its official TUF Store.

One simple activity to get started is solving the Daily Problem of the Day (POTD). Instead of scrolling through social media every day, spend some time solving a coding problem, improve your DSA skills, and collect coins!

Let's understand how it works. 🚀

🔥 What Is the TakeUforward Daily POTD Challenge?

POTD stands for Problem of the Day.

TakeUforward provides a daily coding problem that helps developers practise Data Structures and Algorithms, improve problem-solving skills, and build consistency.

According to the TakeUforward dashboard, the Problem of the Day displays a +20 coin reward.

This means you can make your daily coding practice more rewarding while preparing for technical interviews.

Why should you solve POTD daily?

  • Improve your DSA and problem-solving skills.
  • Build a consistent coding habit.
  • Earn coins through eligible activities.
  • Track your coding progress.
  • Work towards redeeming goodies from the TUF Store.

🪙 How to Earn Coins on TakeUforward?

Follow these simple steps to start collecting coins.

Step 1: Visit TakeUforward

Open the official website:

https://takeuforward.org/

Create an account or log in to your existing account.

Step 2: Find the Problem of the Day

Visit your dashboard and look for the Problem of the Day section.

You can also explore the Practice section to improve your DSA skills.

Step 3: Solve the Daily Coding Problem

  • Open the daily problem.
  • Understand the question carefully.
  • Think about the optimal approach.
  • Write your solution.
  • Test and submit your code.

Try to understand the logic instead of copying solutions. The main goal is to improve your coding skills.

Step 4: Earn Coins

The dashboard currently displays +20 coins for the Problem of the Day.

Complete eligible activities and keep collecting coins in your account.

Step 5: Redeem Your Coins for Goodies

Once you have collected enough coins, visit the official Goodies Store.

Choose an available reward and check whether you have enough coins to redeem it.

1. Is TakeUforward giving free swags?

TakeUforward has a Goodies Store where eligible users can redeem merchandise using earned coins. You need to collect the required coins before redeeming a reward.

The dashboard currently displays a +20 coin reward for the Problem of the Day. Check your account for the applicable reward rules.
