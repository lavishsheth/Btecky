🎁 Can You Actually Attend re for FREE?

Yes, there are AWS-sponsored opportunities.

However, you need to understand which program you're applying to.

AWS currently has an official All Builders Welcome Grant for re 2026.

The program is specifically designed to provide early-career technologists with support to attend re. AWS says a limited number of grants were also available to select university students preparing for technology careers.

What does the grant cover?

According to the official terms, accepted participants receive:

🎟️ Full conference pass

✈️ Round-trip flight

🏨 Hotel accommodation

for the event.

That's significantly more than simply getting a free conference ticket.

⚠️ Watch Out for Fake “FREE re Ticket” Posts

Because a $2,499 conference ticket is expensive, scams around “free tickets” can be attractive.

Be careful if someone asks you to:

❌ Pay a registration fee to unlock a free ticket ❌ Share your password ❌ Provide sensitive financial information ❌ Send money to “claim” an AWS prize ❌ Register through an unknown website ❌ Download suspicious files

A genuine opportunity should have clearly stated eligibility and terms.

When possible, verify the opportunity through AWS's official channels or the official User Group organizing the contest.
