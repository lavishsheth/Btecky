🛠️ What Will You Build During the 15-Day Bootcamp?

The main attraction of this bootcamp is the practical Career Engine you will develop.

Instead of learning isolated AI prompts, you will work towards building a complete system that can help with your job search.

Here are the six major components mentioned in the challenge.

1. Job Search Engine

Finding relevant jobs and internships can be time-consuming.

You may spend hours visiting different job portals, checking company career pages, and comparing opportunities.

With ChatGPT, you will learn to build a Job Search Engine that helps you discover opportunities based on:

  • Your technical skills.
  • Your educational background.
  • Your interests.
  • Your preferred job roles.
  • Your career goals.

For example, if you are a Java developer looking for entry-level opportunities, you can organize your search around Java Developer, Software Engineer, and Backend Developer roles.

The objective is to identify opportunities that are relevant to your profile instead of applying randomly.

2. Job Description Evaluation System

Have you ever found a job opening but struggled to understand whether your profile matches its requirements?

This system helps you evaluate job descriptions and understand how well an opportunity matches your skills and experience.

You can use ChatGPT to:

  • Extract important skills from job descriptions.
  • Identify mandatory and preferred qualifications.
  • Compare job requirements with your resume.
  • Identify missing skills.
  • Understand areas where you need improvement.

For example, if a company asks for Java, Spring Boot, SQL, REST APIs, and React, you can compare these requirements with your existing skills and identify what you should improve before applying.

3. Resume Customization Engine

One of the most important parts of a job application is your resume.

Sending exactly the same resume to every company may not communicate your most relevant experience.

During the bootcamp, you will learn how to use ChatGPT to customize your resume according to specific job descriptions.

You can work on:

  • Highlighting relevant technical skills.
  • Improving project descriptions.
  • Writing stronger experience points.
  • Organizing achievements.
  • Making your resume more relevant to a particular role.

For example, if you are applying for a Java Full Stack Developer position, you can highlight your Java, Spring Boot, REST API, SQL, and frontend experience appropriately.

Always ensure that your resume contains truthful information. AI should help present your real experience better, not create qualifications or achievements you do not have.

4. Portfolio Builder

Your portfolio is a great way to demonstrate your practical skills.

A well-organized portfolio can help recruiters understand what you have built beyond your resume.

Using ChatGPT, you will learn to create a professional portfolio that showcases:

  • Your introduction.
  • Technical skills.
  • Projects.
  • Achievements.
  • Certifications.
  • GitHub profile.
  • Contact information.

You can use the portfolio to present your work in a clear and professional manner.

For developers, showcasing real projects with working demonstrations and GitHub repositories can make the portfolio more valuable.

5. Interview Preparation Engine

Getting shortlisted is only one part of the job search. You also need to prepare for interviews.

The Interview Preparation Engine will help you use ChatGPT to practise and prepare for upcoming interviews.

You can work on:

  • Technical interview questions.
  • HR interview preparation.
  • Role-specific questions.
  • Project explanations.
  • Mock interview practice.
  • Communication and answer improvement.

For example, if you are applying for a Java Developer position, you can practise questions related to Core Java, OOP, Collections, Spring Boot, SQL, and REST APIs.

You can also practise explaining your projects and answering questions about your implementation decisions.

6. Recruiter Outreach Engine

Connecting with recruiters and hiring managers is another important part of the job search.

During the bootcamp, you will learn to create personalized outreach messages using ChatGPT.

You can use this system to prepare:

  • LinkedIn connection requests.
  • Recruiter messages.
  • Job application follow-ups.
  • Professional introductions.
  • Emails expressing interest in opportunities.

Instead of sending generic messages to everyone, you can prepare more relevant communication based on the company and job role.

7. Your Complete AI Career Engine

Finally, you will bring everything together into your own personalized Career Engine.

The idea is to combine the different tools into one organized system that supports your career journey.

Your final Career Engine will cover:

  1. Discovering relevant opportunities.
  2. Evaluating job descriptions.
  3. Customizing your resume.
  4. Building your portfolio.
  5. Preparing for interviews.
  6. Connecting with recruiters.

This can become a useful resource even after the bootcamp ends.
