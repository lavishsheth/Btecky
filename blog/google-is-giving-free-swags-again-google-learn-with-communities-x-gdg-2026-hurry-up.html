☁️ What Is Google Learn With Communities 2026?

Google Cloud Learn With Communities is a community-driven learning initiative that encourages participants to develop practical technical skills through hands-on labs available on Google Skills.

With the support of Google Developer Groups (GDG) on Campus, participants can explore different Google Cloud technologies, complete learning activities, and earn verifiable Google Cloud Skill Badges.

The program focuses on:

  • Google Cloud Computing
  • Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  • Data Technologies
  • Hands-on Cloud Labs
  • Practical Technical Skills
  • Google Cloud Skill Badges

The best part? You can showcase your earned digital badges on your resume and LinkedIn profile!

📋 How to Participate in Google Learn With Communities 2026?

Follow these general steps to get started:

Step 1: Create Your Google Skills Account

Visit Google Skills and sign in using your Google account.

Step 2: Make Your Profile Public

Open your Google Skills profile settings and change your profile visibility to Public.

Copy your public profile URL because you may need it during registration.

Step 3: Complete the Registration Form

Visit the registration link shared by your respective GDG on Campus organizer.

Fill in the required details, including your Google Skills public profile URL.

Step 4: Enter the Correct Referral Code

Make sure you use the referral code provided by the respective GDG chapter when completing registration.

Different GDG chapters may have different referral codes, so don't use someone else's code without checking the official instructions.

Step 5: Wait for Confirmation

After successful enrollment, follow the instructions provided by the organizers. Eligible participants may receive an official email containing their access code for Google Cloud lab credits.

Step 6: Start Completing Your Skill Badges

Complete the eligible Google Skills labs and earn the required Skill Badges during the campaign period.
