How to check your Arcade score on B Tecky (no login)

Step by step: use the free B Tecky Arcade Score Checker to see your points, milestone and rank from your email.

The Arcade Score Checker is a free tool on this website. You type the email you used for the Facilitator program and it shows where you stand. There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to install.

How to use it

  1. Open the Arcade Score Checker.
  2. Type the email address linked to your Google Skills account.
  3. Press Check my score.
  4. Read your estimated total, your milestone and your rank, and see which games and skill badges are counted.

What you will see

  • Your estimated total points, made from games, skill badges and milestone bonuses.
  • Your milestone, and how far you are from the next one.
  • Your rank among everyone in the report.
  • The names of the arcade games and skill badges you have completed.

Good to know

  • Capital letters do not matter, and for Gmail addresses dots and +tags are ignored.
  • Your email is matched inside your own browser. It is never sent to a server or saved.
  • Scores come from a report with a date shown on the page, so newer progress may not appear until the report is refreshed.
  • If your email is not found, check for typos and make sure you joined through the batches in that report.
