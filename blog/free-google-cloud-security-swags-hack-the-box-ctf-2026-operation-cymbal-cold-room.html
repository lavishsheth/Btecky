🛡️ What Is Operation Cymbal: Cold Room?

Operation Cymbal: Cold Room is a cybersecurity Capture The Flag event hosted by Hack The Box in partnership with Google Cloud Security.

The scenario begins with Cymbal Investments, a fictional company that has collapsed after three seemingly unrelated security breaches.

Its environment is sealed inside a virtual "Cold Room," and participants must investigate the evidence to reconstruct the attacks.

Your mission is to:

🔍 Investigate security events and digital evidence.

🛡️ Explore Google Security Operations.

🧩 Reconstruct attacker activity.

🕵️ Connect the different stages of the intrusions.

📊 Prepare defensible findings based on your investigation.

🏁 Complete the CTF challenge before competing investigators.

This is an opportunity to explore security operations and incident investigation through a hands-on challenge.
