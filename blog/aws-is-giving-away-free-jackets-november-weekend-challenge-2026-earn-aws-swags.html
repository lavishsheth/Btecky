🤖 What Is the Once Upon Agentic AI Workshop?

The workshop is titled:

Once Upon Agentic AI: A Developer's Epic Journey

It is a free hands-on learning experience available through AWS Builder Center's sandbox workshops.

The workshop introduces you to Agentic AI and helps you understand how AI agents can perform tasks and collaborate.

What Will You Learn?

During the workshop, you will explore:

  • Building your first Strands agent.
  • Understanding how AI agents work.
  • Exploring agentic AI workflows.
  • Building a multi-agent system.
  • Understanding how multiple agents can collaborate.
  • Applying agentic AI to practical development problems.

The objective is to move beyond simply understanding AI and actually experiment with building AI agents.

🧠 What Is Strands Agents?

Strands Agents is an open-source framework for building AI agents.

It helps developers create agents that can reason, use tools, and perform tasks.

Using Strands, you can experiment with:

  • Personal AI assistants.
  • Research agents.
  • Coding assistants.
  • Productivity automation.
  • Multi-agent applications.
  • Intelligent workflow automation.

This workshop provides an opportunity to explore these concepts through practical exercises.

Step 1: Visit AWS Builder Center

Step 2: Open the Free Workshops Section

Step 5: Visit the Official Weekend Challenge Article

Step 6: Share What You Learned in the Comments

This is the most important step!

You need to comment on the official challenge article and share:

Your Builder Center profile must include all four of the following:

Step 8: Submit Before the Deadline

Post your qualifying comment within the official challenge window.

Once you've completed all the requirements, you will be entered into the two prize drawings.
