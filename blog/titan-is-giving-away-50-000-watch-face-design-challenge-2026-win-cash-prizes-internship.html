🔥 What Is the Titan Watch Face Design Challenge 2026?

The Titan Watch Face Design Challenge 2026 is a design competition powered by Hack2skill.

The objective is to encourage creative individuals to design original and innovative smartwatch faces that improve how people interact with wearable technology.

A smartwatch face is more than just a screen displaying the time. It is the primary interface through which users interact with their watches.

A good watch face should combine:

  • Attractive visual design.
  • Clear time display.
  • Easy readability.
  • Useful information.
  • Good colour combinations.
  • A user-friendly layout.

Through this challenge, Titan wants participants to rethink how time, information, and design come together on a smartwatch.

You can experiment with different styles, layouts, typography, colours, and creative concepts.

The theme is Open Innovation, which means you have the freedom to explore your own design direction.

Important Watch Face Design Requirements

Before starting your design, make sure you understand these important instructions.

1. Time Is Mandatory

Your watch face must display the time.

Other information is optional, including:

  • Date.
  • Day.
  • Weather.
  • Battery percentage.
  • Steps.
  • Heart rate.

You can decide which additional information makes sense for your design.

2. Keep Important Information Inside the Inner Circle

The challenge specifically recommends keeping time and data inside the inner circle.

You should use the outer ring only for decorative elements.

This is important because the main information needs to remain clear and readable.

3. Follow the 60-30-10 Colour Rule

A useful design recommendation is the 60-30-10 colour rule.

PercentageUsage
60%Main Colour
30%Secondary Colour
10%Accent Colour

For example, you could use:

  • 60% black or dark background.
  • 30% grey or another supporting colour.
  • 10% bright blue or another accent colour.

This approach helps create a balanced design without making the watch face look overcrowded.

4. Use Commercially Free Fonts

Make sure the fonts you use are free for commercial use.

Do not use fonts that have licensing restrictions preventing commercial usage.

5. Do Not Add Any Logo

The challenge instructions clearly state that you should not add any logo to your design.

Focus on your original design, typography, layout, colours, and visual elements.

How to Approach Your Design

If you are confused about where to start, follow this simple design workflow.

Step 1: Understand the User

Think about who will use your watch face.

For example:

  • Someone who prefers minimal designs.
  • Someone who wants quick access to fitness information.
  • Someone who prefers colourful and expressive interfaces.
  • Someone who wants a simple and readable time display.

Step 2: Sketch Your Concept

Before opening your design software, create a rough layout.

Decide where the time, date, and optional information should appear.

Step 3: Select Your Colour Palette

Choose a primary colour, secondary colour, and accent colour.

Try to maintain good contrast so the information is easy to read.

Step 4: Create Your Design

Use Adobe or Figma to create your smartwatch face.

Focus on the overall composition, spacing, typography, and readability.

Step 5: Review the Design

Check whether:

  • Time is clearly visible.
  • Important data remains inside the inner circle.
  • The outer ring is used only for decoration.
  • Colours are balanced.
  • Fonts are commercially permitted.
  • No logo has been added.

Step 6: Prepare Your Submission

Review the official submission instructions and upload your design in the required format.

🏆 How Will Your Design Be Evaluated?

According to the official challenge page, submissions will be reviewed using four major criteria.

1. Originality and Uniqueness of Concept

Your design should demonstrate creativity and a unique approach.

Avoid simply copying existing smartwatch faces.

Try to develop an original visual identity and concept.

2. Visual Design

The overall appearance of your watch face matters.

This includes:

  • Colour combinations.
  • Typography.
  • Visual hierarchy.
  • Layout.
  • Spacing.
  • Overall aesthetics.

3. Usability

A smartwatch face should be practical, not just attractive.

Users should be able to understand the displayed information quickly.

The time should be easy to read, and optional information should not make the interface confusing.

4. Quality and Completeness of Submission

Your final submission should be complete and properly prepared.

Make sure your design follows the challenge instructions and includes all the required elements.

Tip: Don't focus only on making your watch face look beautiful. A strong design should combine originality, visual appeal, and everyday usability.
