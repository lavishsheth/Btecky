HackerRank is back with its December 2026 edition of Orchestrate – Holiday Edition, a global 24-hour hackathon where developers get the chance to build, test, and submit their own AI agents.

🎯 What Is HackerRank Orchestrate December 2026?

HackerRank Orchestrate is a 24-hour AI hackathon where participants receive a real-world problem and must design, build, and submit an AI agent to solve it.

You can use your preferred AI coding tools and development environment, including:

  • Claude Code
  • Cursor
  • OpenAI Codex
  • Other AI tools of your choice

The challenge is not just about generating code. You must also understand your solution and explain the decisions behind it during an AI-powered interview.

Event: Orchestrate – Holiday Edition Platform: HackerRank Format: Online, individual participation Duration: 24 hours Start Date: December 12, 2026

🚀 How to Participate in HackerRank December Challenge 2026?

Follow these steps to join the challenge.

Step 1: Visit the Official Registration Page

Open the official HackerRank Orchestrate December 2026 page:

Register for HackerRank Orchestrate December 2026

Step 2: Register for the Challenge

  • Sign in to your HackerRank Community account.
  • Complete the registration process.
  • Submit the required details.
  • Check your registered email for confirmation.

Step 3: Get Ready for the Challenge

Before the event, prepare your preferred AI coding tool.

You can use tools such as Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex. Make sure your development environment and internet connection are ready.

Step 4: Build Your AI Agent

On December 12, you will receive the real-world problem statement by email.

You will have 24 hours to:

  • Understand the problem.
  • Plan your AI agent.
  • Build and test your solution.
  • Prepare the required submission files.

Step 5: Submit Your Project

You will need to submit:

  1. Code ZIP file: Your agent's source code and README.
  2. Output file: The generated output in the format specified by the challenge.
  3. AI Chat Transcript: Your conversation history with the AI coding tool.

Follow the instructions in your challenge email and submit before the deadline.

Step 6: Face the AI Judge

After submitting, you can complete a 30-minute voice-based AI interview.

The AI interviewer may ask about:

  • Your agent's architecture.
  • How your solution works.
  • Information retrieval and tool usage.
  • Safety risks and failure handling.
  • Testing and evaluation.
  • Your implementation decisions.

Your solution and your ability to explain it both matter!
