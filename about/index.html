About B Tecky

People, ideas and technology—built with purpose.

B Tecky is an early-stage technology venture creating practical digital tools, learning resources and software experiences. We are starting with focused products, learning in public and improving based on real needs.

Founder & CEO • Development Lead

Lavish Sheth

I lead B Tecky’s vision, product direction and development. My interests span software engineering, web experiences, product thinking and building practical tools around real user needs.

B Tecky began as a way to share technology learning and has grown into a broader foundation for product and service ideas. I care about shipping useful work, learning in public, collaborating with people who build, and turning early concepts into reliable experiences.
